    <section class="rackets">

      <article class="card">
        <h2>Head-Heavy Racket</h2>
        <p>Built for power. The weight sits towards the top of the frame, so every smash and clear hits harder.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Material: High-Modulus Carbon Graphite</li>
          <li>Best for: attacking and back-court players</li>
          <li>Strength: powerful smashes and deep clears</li>
          <li>Trade-off: slower in fast exchanges</li>
        </ul>
      </article>

      <article class="card blue">
        <h2>Balanced Racket</h2>
        <p>Built for all-round play. The weight is spread evenly, so the racket feels quick and easy to control.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Material: High-Modulus Carbon Graphite</li>
          <li>Best for: doubles and all-round players</li>
          <li>Strength: fast defence and quick drives</li>
          <li>Trade-off: slightly less smash power</li>
        </ul>
      </article>

    </section>

    <section class="tension">
      <h2>String Tension</h2>
      <p class="big">30 lbs</p>
      <p>Competitive players choose 30 lbs string tension for crisp control. The tighter strings give a clean, sharp feel on every shot, so placement at the net and in drop shots becomes more accurate.</p>
      <p>It is a great balance of control and power, and it is why many club players string their rackets at this level.</p>
